The Problem

You have sat across from sponsors who made commitments they never intended to keep. Equity that shifted after close. Partners who went silent when it mattered. Exit terms that looked nothing like the conversation at hire.

That intelligence exists. It travels through private networks, over dinner, between people who know the right people. It is inaccessible to most of the executives who need it most.

Fleming changes that.

What Fleming Is

Fleming is an invite-only intelligence platform. Verified, anonymous accounts of PE sponsor conduct from the executives and operators who were actually in the room.

First-hand. Structured. Protected.

Contributors are portco CEOs, CFOs, COOs, and equity-holding VPs who have lived the experience and are willing to put it on record. On their own terms.

Your Protection Is Architecture, Not Policy

Fleming cannot connect a review to a contributor identity. Not by policy. By design. Identity records and review content are stored in completely separate systems with no shared key. The platform cannot produce contributor identity under compulsion because it does not possess it.

  • Anonymous alias assigned immediately after verification
  • Review held until a minimum of three verified accounts exist for the same sponsor. No solo exposure.
  • You review and approve your anonymized draft before publication
  • Removal standard is published. Reviews are not removed based on sponsor objection alone.
  • Legal challenges are tracked and published as platform data.
